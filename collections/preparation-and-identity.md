# Ontology, identity, and document preparation

These components improve the knowledge entering an application: ontology editors express definitions, entity resolution reconciles identities, and parsers produce document content. Their outputs are complementary. Accurate parsing does not establish factual truth, and assigning an entity type does not establish which real-world entity a mention identifies.

## Tool profiles

### [Docling](../tools/docling.md)

Document parsing, structured representation, OCR options, and chunking supporting graph ingestion.

### [Protégé / WebProtégé](../tools/protege-webprotege.md)

OWL ontology authoring supporting formal concept and relationship definitions for a graph application.

### [Senzing](../tools/senzing.md)

Entity resolution helping reconcile fragmented records and relationships before or during graph population.

### [Unstructured](../tools/unstructured.md)

Document parsing and structured extraction supporting the content entering graph construction and retrieval.

## Compare complete responsibilities

Use the linked profiles to distinguish definitions, populated facts, evidence retrieval, and answering. Then test identity, relationship provenance, freshness, citation support, and the actual graph/schema integration.
